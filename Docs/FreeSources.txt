  │ Hosting       │ Hugging Face Spaces │ free, easy demo link for your resume                                 │
  └───────────────┴─────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  
  Caveats to plan around

  - All of these are rate-limited, not unlimited — fine for demos/interviews, not for sustained heavy traffic. Don't design load tests around them.
  - HF Spaces free CPU tier will feel slow for a multi-agent chain (multiple sequential LLM calls) — expect a few seconds per response, which is normal and fine for a demo.
  - Google's Gemini free tier has reportedly had its quotas cut sharply (50–80%) in recent months, so it's less stable than Groq if you're relying on it for a live-linked demo.
