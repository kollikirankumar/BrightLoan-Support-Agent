# About Brightloan

## Is Brightloan a Legitimate, Regulated Company?

Yes. Brightloan operates as an NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company)
under RBI (Reserve Bank of India) regulations applicable to NBFC-ND-SI
entities — the same regulatory framework referenced in the Grievance
Redressal document. All lending practices, interest rate disclosures,
and recovery conduct rules follow RBI's Fair Practices Code.

## What Does Brightloan Do?

Brightloan is a digital lending company offering small-ticket personal
loans to individuals and working-capital business loans (invoice
discounting) to vendors and suppliers — see Loan Offers & Terms for the
full details of both.

## How Is My Money and Data Protected?

Loan agreements are e-signed and legally binding under Indian law.
Personal and financial data is handled per the Privacy Policy, with
industry-standard encryption and no sale of personal data to third
parties for marketing. All fees and charges are disclosed upfront in
the loan agreement before e-signing — nothing is charged that wasn't
shown to you first.

## How Can I Verify a Brightloan Communication Is Genuine?

Official communication only comes through the Brightloan app, the
registered support email/phone (see Contact Information), or verified
in-app channels. If you're ever unsure whether a call, SMS, or email
claiming to be from Brightloan is genuine, contact Customer Care
directly to verify before sharing any information.
