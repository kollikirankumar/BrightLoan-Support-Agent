# Frequently Asked Questions

## What kind of loans do you provide?

Two types: Personal Loans (small-ticket digital loans for individuals,
₹5,000 to ₹1,00,000) and Business Loans (invoice discounting for
vendors and suppliers needing working capital). See Loan Offers & Terms
for the full details of each.

## Is Brightloan a real, registered company?

Yes — Brightloan operates as an RBI-regulated NBFC, following the same
Fair Practices Code and grievance framework required of all regulated
lenders in India. See About Brightloan for more.

## Can I still get a loan with a bad credit score?

Yes — a low credit score doesn't automatically disqualify you. Approval
also weighs income stability and repayment capacity from your bank
statement. A lower score typically means a higher interest rate within
our range, not automatic rejection. See Loan Offers & Terms for details.

## What do I need to apply?

Just three things: Aadhaar, PAN, and a bank account with net banking
enabled. No salary slips or ITRs — see the Loan Application Process
document for the full step-by-step.

## How long does the whole process take?

Pre-approval is instant. Most applicants finish the entire process —
from download to e-signing — in about 10 minutes, with funds disbursed
the same day.

## What happens if I miss an EMI?

A grace period of 3 days applies. After that, a late fee is charged —
see the Company Policy document for the exact amount. Missing 3
consecutive EMIs may lead to a collections referral and credit bureau
reporting.

## Can I increase my loan amount after applying?

Not on an existing application. Applicants can apply for a top-up loan
after 6 months of on-time repayment on their current loan, subject to a
fresh eligibility check.

## Do you offer loans to NRIs?

No. Brightloan currently only offers loans to Indian residents with a
valid PAN and Aadhaar.

## How is my interest rate decided?

Rates are benchmarked to leading banks' credit card interest rates and
vary by individual risk profile — see the Loan Offers & Terms document
for how this is calculated.

## I received a call from someone claiming to be a Brightloan recovery agent — how do I verify it's real?

Genuine recovery agents carry ID issued by their agency and Brightloan,
and will identify themselves and the purpose of the call immediately.
If in doubt, don't share any information — hang up and call Customer
Care directly to verify. See the Collection Agencies document for the
full conduct rules agents must follow.

## Can I cancel a loan after taking it?

Yes, but only within 24 hours of e-signing the agreement, and the
request must be in writing to Customer Care. After that window, it's
handled as regular repayment, not cancellation. See the Cancellation
Policy document for details.

## Is my personal data shared with anyone?

Only as needed to service your loan — credit bureaus, KYC/verification
agencies, and, for overdue accounts, authorized collection agencies.
Brightloan does not sell personal data for marketing. See the Privacy
Policy document for the full list.

## How do I file a complaint?

Start with Customer Care (see Contact Information) or the Grievance
Redressal Officer directly. Resolution timelines depend on the
complaint type — 10 to 30 working days — and unresolved complaints can
be escalated further, up to RBI's Department of Non-Banking Supervision.
See the Grievance Redressal document for the complete process.
