# Loan Offers & Terms

## Loan Types We Offer

Brightloan offers two types of loans: **Personal Loans** — small-ticket
digital loans for individuals — and **Business Loans** — working
capital financing for vendors and suppliers, provided via invoice
discounting against unpaid invoices.

## Personal Loans (Small-Ticket Digital Loans)

Amount range: ₹5,000 to ₹1,00,000. Tenure: 3 to 12 months. Interest
rates are benchmarked to the credit card interest rates of leading
banks, and vary by individual risk profile rather than a single fixed
rate — your specific rate is shown in the loan offer before you e-sign,
so the exact cost is always clear upfront.

## Business Loans (Invoice Discounting for Vendors & Suppliers)

A separate product aimed at business vendors/suppliers needing working
capital against unpaid invoices. Interest rate: 14% to 24% APR,
depending on risk profile.

## What Determines Your Rate

Rates are set using a graded risk approach based on: your profile and
repayment history, the loan product and purpose, income/repayment
capacity as verified from your bank statement, and applicable
regulatory requirements. The underlying pricing accounts for the cost
of funds, operating expenses, and a risk premium — which is why rates
vary meaningfully between applicants rather than being a single flat
number.

## Credit Score & Eligibility

A low credit score does not automatically disqualify an applicant —
approval is based on a broader risk assessment that includes income
stability and repayment capacity from your bank statement, not credit
score alone. A lower credit score typically results in a higher
interest rate within our range rather than automatic rejection.
First-time borrowers with little or no credit history are evaluated
primarily on bank statement and income data.

## Other Charges

Any processing fee, late payment charge, or other cost applicable to
your specific loan is disclosed in your loan agreement before you
e-sign — nothing is charged that isn't shown upfront in that agreement.
