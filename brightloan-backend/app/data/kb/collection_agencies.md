# Collection Agencies

## When Accounts Are Referred to Collections

Accounts with 3 or more consecutive missed EMIs may be referred to an
authorized third-party collection agency for recovery, in addition to
the late fees described in the Company Policy document. Referral to
collections does not remove the borrower's ability to resolve the
matter directly with Brightloan at any point.

## Authorized Agencies

Brightloan works only with collection agencies registered under
applicable recovery-agent regulations. Every recovery agent must carry
an identity card issued by the agency and Brightloan, and must produce
it on request before discussing the account. Borrowers should treat any
recovery contact that cannot produce valid identification as suspicious
and report it immediately.

## Permitted Conduct

Recovery agents acting on Brightloan's behalf are required to:

- Contact borrowers only between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
- Identify themselves, the agency they represent, and the purpose of the
  call at the start of every contact.
- Communicate in a civil, non-threatening manner at all times.
- Contact only the borrower or their nominated co-applicant/guarantor —
  never third parties (family, employer, neighbors) except to obtain
  contact information for the borrower.

## Prohibited Conduct

Recovery agents are strictly prohibited from: visiting a borrower's
residence or workplace outside reasonable hours, using threatening or
abusive language, misrepresenting the amount owed, or publicly
disclosing a borrower's debt status to any third party.

## Reporting Misconduct

Any recovery conduct that violates the rules above should be reported
immediately through the Grievance Redressal process (Level 1 or
directly to Level 2 for urgent cases). Verified misconduct results in
the agency being suspended from handling Brightloan accounts pending
investigation.
