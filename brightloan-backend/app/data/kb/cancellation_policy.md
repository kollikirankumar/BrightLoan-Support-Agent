# Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window

A loan can be cancelled by submitting a request **within 24 hours of
executing (e-signing) the loan agreement**. This is a strict window —
requests after 24 hours are not treated as cancellations and instead
fall under normal repayment/foreclosure terms per your loan agreement.

## How to Cancel

Cancellation must be requested in writing, by emailing Customer Care
(see Contact Information) within the 24-hour window. Verbal or in-app
requests alone are not sufficient — a written request is required.

## Cancellation Charges

Any applicable cancellation charge is calculated from the date
Brightloan receives your written request, and follows the terms set out
in your specific loan agreement. There isn't a single flat fee — check
your agreement for the exact amount applicable to your loan.

## Refund Timeline

If a refund is due as part of cancellation, it typically takes 2-4
weeks to process, due to standard banking clearance procedures.

## After the Cancellation Window

Once the 24-hour window has passed, a loan can no longer be cancelled
outright. At that point, closing the loan early is handled as a normal
repayment matter under your loan agreement's terms, not as a
cancellation.
