# Grievance Redressal

Brightloan follows a three-tier grievance process aligned with RBI
guidelines applicable to NBFCs, covering assessment, registration,
escalation, and resolution of complaints.

## How to Register a Complaint

Complaints can be filed through the Brightloan website, by email, in
person at the registered office, or directly with the Grievance
Redressal Officer (see below).

## Resolution Timelines by Category

Response time depends on the nature of the complaint:

- **Standard complaints**: 10 working days
- **Fraud or legal matters**: 15 working days
- **EMI-related issues**: 20 working days
- **Complaints involving a third party** (e.g. a collection agency):
  30 working days

## Level 1: Grievance Redressal Officer

Email: grievance@brightloan.example
Phone/WhatsApp: see Contact Information
Hours: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Monday–Saturday (excluding holidays)

## Level 2: Director Escalation

If unresolved at Level 1, complaints can be escalated to Brightloan's
Director via director@brightloan.example, with a response expected
within 15 working days.

## Level 3: RBI Regulatory Escalation

If a complaint remains unresolved after one month, it can be escalated
to RBI's Department of Non-Banking Supervision (Chennai region):

General Manager, RBI Department of Non-Banking Supervision
Fort Glacis, Rajaji Salai, Chennai 600 001
Phone: 044 2539 3406
Email: dnbschennai@rbi.org.in

## Complaints About Collection Conduct

Complaints specifically about recovery-agent behavior are treated as
priority escalations — see the Collection Agencies document for the
conduct rules those agents must follow.
