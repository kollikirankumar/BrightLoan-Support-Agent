# Company Policy

## Prepayment & Foreclosure

Given the short tenures (3–12 months) on Brightloan's digital personal
loans, prepayment can be made at any time during the loan term. Any
applicable foreclosure charge is disclosed in your specific loan
agreement before you e-sign — there isn't a single flat rate across all
loans, since it depends on the product and tenure.

## Late Payment

A grace period of 3 days is provided after the EMI due date. The late
payment fee is 2% of the overdue EMI amount, or ₹500, whichever is
higher. Missing 3 consecutive EMIs may result in the account being
referred to collections and reported to credit bureaus.

## Cancellation

See the Cancellation Policy document for full details, including the
24-hour cancellation window after e-signing the loan agreement.
