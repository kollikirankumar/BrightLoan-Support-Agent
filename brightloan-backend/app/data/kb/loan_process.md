# Loan Application Process

Brightloan has two separate application processes, one for personal loans
and one for business loans, since they work very differently.

## Personal Loan — Required Documents

Applying for a Brightloan personal loan requires only three things:

1. **Aadhaar Card** — government-issued identity proof.
2. **PAN Card** — tax identification.
3. **A bank account with net banking enabled** — used for both bank
   statement verification and disbursement.

No salary slips, ITRs, or physical paperwork are required — the entire
process is digital and document-light by design.

## Personal Loan — Step-by-Step Process

Here is the full process to apply for a Brightloan personal loan, from
application to disbursement:

1. Download the Brightloan app from the Play Store.
2. Enter your mobile number for automatic OTP verification.
3. Provide PAN, PIN code, and date of birth.
4. Receive an instant pre-approval decision.
5. Upload your bank statement via net banking OTP (no manual document
   upload needed).
6. Review your loan amount, tenure, and agreement terms.
7. E-sign the agreement using Aadhaar-based verification and a selfie.
8. Set up an e-NACH mandate for automatic EMI deduction.
9. Funds are credited to your registered bank account.

Pre-approval is instant (seconds), and the full application — from
download to e-signing — typically takes about 10 minutes. Disbursement
happens the same day, directly to your bank account.

## Business Loan — Required Documents

Applying for a Brightloan business loan (invoice discounting) requires:

1. **GST registration certificate** and **business PAN**.
2. **Bank statements** showing the business's transaction history.
3. **The unpaid invoice(s)** you want financed against, along with basic
   buyer/debtor details.

## Business Loan — Step-by-Step Process

Here is the full process to apply for a Brightloan business loan, from
application to disbursement:

1. Register as a vendor/supplier on the Brightloan business portal,
   providing your GST and business PAN details.
2. Upload the unpaid invoice(s) you want financed against.
3. Brightloan verifies the invoice and the buyer/debtor relationship.
4. Receive an offer specifying the advance rate and applicable interest
   (14%–24% APR, based on risk profile).
5. Accept the offer and e-sign the invoice discounting agreement.
6. A percentage of the invoice value is disbursed to your business bank
   account, usually within 1–2 business days of verification.
7. When the buyer settles the invoice, the remaining balance (minus fees)
   is paid out to you.

Business loan disbursement takes 1–2 business days rather than being
instant, since invoice and buyer verification requires manual checks that
personal loans don't need.

## Eligibility

There's no separate manual eligibility check for personal loans —
approval is decided instantly based on the details and bank statement
provided during the application itself. For business loans, eligibility
depends on invoice and buyer verification. In both cases, Brightloan
reserves the right to decline any application at its discretion.
