# Privacy Policy

## Scope

Applies to all users of Brightloan's website, mobile app, and
WhatsApp-based channels, covering personal and sensitive personal
information collected online, offline, or via mobile device.

## Information We Collect

- **Personal/financial data** you provide directly: name, date of
  birth, address, income, bank statements, PAN, Aadhaar.
- **Device and browser data** collected automatically (device ID, OS
  version, app version, clickstream/traffic data).
- **Contact list access** (Android, optional) — used only to support
  alternate-contact verification during underwriting; can be denied,
  though it may affect processing time.
- **Profiling information** such as age, gender, and income bracket.
- **Location data**, where enabled — see Location Services below.

## How We Use Information

Collected information is used only for purposes incidental to
Brightloan's loan services: eligibility assessment, identity/address
verification, loan servicing (reminders, statements), fraud detection,
marketing communication about products, and improving the app
experience.

## Cookies

Brightloan uses persistent cookies to store non-personal preference and
profiling data. Personal information — name, account number, contact
details — is never stored in cookies.

## Location Services

Location is accessed only while the app is actively in use (never in
the background), and only to verify that an applicant's address falls
within Brightloan's currently serviceable areas. Location access can be
denied, though manual address entry may then be required and some
features may be unavailable.

## Third-Party Sharing

Information may be shared with Brightloan-affiliated entities,
third-party agents/agencies needed to deliver a service, credit
bureaus, and, for overdue accounts, authorized collection agencies (see
Collection Agencies). Third parties are contractually restricted from
using shared data for unrelated purposes.

## Security

Brightloan uses commercially reasonable measures to protect data in
transit and at rest, but — as with any online system — cannot guarantee
absolute security. No transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

## External Links

Brightloan has no control over, and no responsibility for, the content
or privacy practices of any third-party website linked from its
platform.

## Your Responsibilities

You're responsible for verifying the legitimacy of any contact claiming
to be from Brightloan before sharing sensitive information — official
verification is always available via Customer Care (see Contact
Information). You're also encouraged to: check for "https" in your
browser's address bar, avoid unauthorized browser extensions, keep
cookies disabled unless you've consciously enabled them, avoid
suspicious or unofficial download sources, and never click unidentified
links claiming to be from Brightloan.

## Policy Updates

This policy may be updated at any time, with changes taking effect
immediately upon posting. Users are advised to review it periodically.
